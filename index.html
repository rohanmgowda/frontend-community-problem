About Us

Local issues often go unreported or untracked. We built a single place where citizens describe problems with text, photos and an optional location, and where officials verify, assign and update each case.

Reports are not treated as verified facts until reviewed, and a complaint is never shown as resolved without a recorded update.

Features

Smart categorisation

A locally trained TF-IDF model predicts the category and shows its confidence.

Visual tracking

A progress timeline from Submitted to Resolved with administrator remarks.

Duplicate detection

Cosine similarity flags repeated reports so teams don't double-handle.

Live analytics

Category, status, monthly and area-wise charts from real records.

Department workflow

Assign to Road Maintenance, Sanitation, Electrical, Water Supply and more.

Notifications

Citizens are told when a complaint is verified, assigned, started or resolved.
